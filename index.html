Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free, but booking online guarantees your preferred stylist and time — especially on weekends.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Absolutely. Choose "Any available stylist" for the fastest slot, or pick a named stylist during booking if you have a preference.

What if I need to cancel or change my time?

Use "Manage booking" in the top menu with your reference code and email to cancel any time before your appointment.

Do you take card payments?

Yes — we accept all major cards and contactless payment in-store. Payment is taken after your service, not at booking.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 5 minutes before your slot. Running more than 10 minutes late may mean your appointment needs to be rescheduled.
